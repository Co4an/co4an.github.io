MOEX & SPBEX Quant Advisor

Public code

Open-Source Quantitative Equity Terminal without Paid Data Feeds

Repository: github.com/Co4an/moex-spb-quant-advisor · Live terminal: co4an.github.io/moex-spb-quant-advisor

A zero-dependency Python pipeline and interactive web terminal ingesting live market feeds from Moscow Exchange (510 TQBR stocks, IMOEX, RGBI) and SPB Exchange (247 instruments), Bank of Russia historical key-rate series and FX feeds, and IFRS/RAS filings for 31 issuers across 8 sectors. PolarGate

Research manuscript & modeling suite

Applied Graph Modeling of Collective Dynamics & Roll-Call Forecasting

PyTorch · Python · LaTeX/PDF · private research project

Research forecasting architecture evaluated strictly out-of-time across historical roll calls, delivering a +0.1420 Macro-F1 gain across 528 held-out 117th U.S. Congress votes (0.5731 vs 0.4311 for Unsigned GNN). Over 360 simulation steps, explicit Friedkin–Johnsen damping preserves opinion dispersion at 0.230 vs 0.247 ground truth at 0.0032 ms/step latency, whereas Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct loses 33.4% down to 0.165. Internal architecture and source code are proprietary.
